To increase the earlier nine-view result from 77.22\% background coverage, we added a 95\% goal using the same nine required IDs and 41-sample weights, and selected a twelve-view hybrid ADP plan. Synchronous screening rejected views 5 and 12 because their endpoints lay inside the unchanged caution zone. We shifted them by \unit{8}{m} and \unit{6}{m}, adjusted their camera aims, and recomputed all transfers and visibility masks. The observation order, camera criteria, safety settings and acceptance tolerances were retained, with \unit{60}{s} settling after each \unit{90}{s} transfer. This post-hoc case, its failed diagnostic and refinement search are archived separately from the unchanged held-out study.

The subsequent normal-speed graphical run used Ubuntu~24.04.4, ROS~2 Jazzy, Gazebo Harmonic, RViz2, and Python~3.12.3. All twelve observations passed at \unit{@TIME@}{s}, accepting 9/9 required targets and 95.86\% weighted background coverage (39/41 inspectable samples). Maximum terminal position error and speed were \unit{@POS@}{m} and \unit{@SPEED@}{m/s}.

Integrating $@CONTROLS@$ timestamped safe-control messages through the final event gave \unit{@DV@}{m/s} executed $\Delta v$, versus \unit{@PLANDV@}{m/s} for the transfer-and-hold reference. The safety stream reported $@FILTER@$ interventions. Auditing $@STATES@$ trajectory samples and their connecting segments against $247\,525$ transformed triangles found no surface crossing. Minimum swept-body clearance was \unit{@MARGIN@}{m} above the required \unit{2}{m} margin; peak acceleration was $0.0600~\mathrm{m/s^2}$. All execution and full-mesh audit gates passed. Reference-count completion passed, although the publisher reported a maximum \unit{0.249}{s} gap, exceeding the \unit{0.075}{s} nominal timing diagnostic.
