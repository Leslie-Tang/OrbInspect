A pre-revision copy is in `archive/references_expansion_20260908/` (excluded from the source ZIP).

## Metadata decisions and exclusions

- `kimura2000inspection`: Crossref lists the authors in reverse order. The author institution lists Kimura first. The [researcher's university publication record](https://www.tus.ac.jp/ridai/doc/ji/RIJIA01.php?act=ter&dis=30&fda=1&fmo=1&fye=2006&kin=pap&option=&page=226&radio=&sl2=&sl3=&sl4=&sl5=&tda=1&tmo=6&tye=2009) gives the second surname as **Okuyama**, whereas the aggregated institutional/Crossref records contain **Okyuama**. The author-controlled order and spelling are used.
- `tweddle2016facility`: online publication was October 2015, but the assigned issue is March 2016, volume 33(2). The bibliography uses 2016.
- `bertsekas2017iterations`: uses the published IEEE title, not the differing working-paper title.
- The old `ogri2025switched` entry and its switched-system/dwell sentence were removed. Search records for its arXiv identifier showed inconsistent titles and author lists. The published ACC paper on monocular inspection was not silently treated as the same work. The added van Wijk journal article supplies a separately verified, direct spacecraft-inspection DRL reference.
- The TAES article with DOI `10.1109/TAES.2025.3530392` has verified bibliographic metadata, but its primary abstract/full text was not accessible in this session. It was not added from its title or secondary summaries. The accessible Wang et al. 2024 inspection-control paper supports the corresponding discussion instead.
- The original RRT technical report, foundational books, and NASA geometry source remain because they identify original methods or the actual model asset. They are not arXiv substitutes or reference-count padding.
- The 1997 rollout paper and the existing 2020 monograph anchor the policy-improvement attribution. The manuscript does not claim that generic rollout feasibility or no-degradation was invented here. Its theory is presented as a specialization to required-target SOOA sequencing.
