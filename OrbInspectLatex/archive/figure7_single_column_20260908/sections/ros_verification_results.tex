\subsection{Historical Corrected ROS 2 Survey Execution}
\label{sec:ros-verification-results}

The historical 80\%-survey validation task ran on Ubuntu~24.04.4 with ROS~2 Jazzy,
Gazebo Harmonic, and RViz2. Gazebo supplied the station/chaser rendering and
camera image only; the ROS-native HCW node remained the state source of truth.
The input route was reconstructed from the corrected 24-node graph using the
complete glTF scene hierarchy and a conservative
$r_{\mathrm{veh}}=\unit{0.80}{m}$ chaser bounding radius. The route manifest
records 10 planned SOOAs, \unit{900}{s} mission duration, 81.33\% planned
coverage, and \unit{2.808}{m} minimum planned finite-body clearance above the
required \unit{2}{m} margin.

The $5\times$ graphical closed-loop execution credited all 10 terminal
observations, reached 81.33\% coverage, and completed at mission time
\unit{900.01}{s} without a failed action. The post-run audit evaluated $18\,918$
recorded trajectory samples and every intervening segment against all $247\,525$
fully transformed ISS triangles. No segment crossed the mesh. The smallest
sampled center distance was \unit{8.506}{m}; after the continuous segment lower
bound and the \unit{0.80}{m} vehicle radius, the minimum body distance was
\unit{7.692}{m}, or \unit{5.692}{m} above the required safety margin. Peak
filtered acceleration was $0.0600~\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Terminal, reference-stream,
data-presence, acceleration, full-mesh, swept-crossing, and finite-body gates
all passed.

Fig.~\ref{fig:ros-key-camera-views} links the ten synchronized camera frames
to their credited positions in two orthogonal projections of the executed trajectory.
The evidence directory contains six CSV streams, configuration and route manifests,
the launch log, mesh audit, raw RViz and camera streams, and a 198.3-s annotated video.
