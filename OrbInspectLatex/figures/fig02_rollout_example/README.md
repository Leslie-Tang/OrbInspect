# Figure 2: compact single-column rollout example

`adp_rollout_mechanism.drawio` is the current native editable source, with
matching PDF, SVG and PNG exports. The canvas is 800 by 920, approximately
84.35 by 97.00 mm at the manuscript's single-column width. All information,
fonts, colors and equations are unchanged from the approved compact version.

The three panels show the audited graph, rollout decision and retained
completion. `illustrative_graph.json` records the educational example: base
cost 8, rollout cost 4 and retained tail cost 3. These assigned values are
not experimental HCW data. `QA.json` preserves the preceding export checks.

The earlier wide and taller single-column alternatives remain under
`archive/figure2_alternatives/`, relative to the manuscript root. They are
excluded from the lightweight standalone source ZIP.
